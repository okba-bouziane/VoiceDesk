# Preparing for your visit

> Sample data: fictional clinic, invented guidance.

## Fasting for blood tests
For most blood tests fasting is needed: do not eat for 8 to 10 hours beforehand. Water is allowed. Take your usual medicines unless the doctor says otherwise.

## Medical certificates and documents
Medical certificates are issued during the consultation, free of charge for patients seen at the clinic. Copies of medical files are ready within three working days and require an identity document.

## Test results
Laboratory results are sent to the doctor within two working days. The doctor shares them during a follow-up visit or by phone. The clinic does not give results by email.

## Prescriptions
Prescription renewals for long-term treatments need a consultation at least once every six months. The clinic does not issue prescriptions by phone.

## Vaccinations
Routine adult and child vaccinations are available by appointment. Travel vaccines such as yellow fever are not given here.

## Privacy
Medical information is shared only with the patient, or with the parent or guardian of a child. The voice assistant stores only the name, phone number and appointment details needed to manage a booking.
