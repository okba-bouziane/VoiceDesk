# Opening hours and location

> Sample data: fictional clinic, invented details.

## Opening hours
Riad Sample Clinic is open Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 to 16:30. Appointments are bookable from 9:00 to 12:00 and from 13:00 to 16:00. The clinic is closed on Fridays and Saturdays and on national public holidays.

## Lunch break
There is a lunch break from 12:00 to 13:00 every open day. The reception desk is staffed during the break, but no appointments are scheduled.

## Address and access
The clinic is on the second floor of the Sample Tower, 12 Example Street, Algiers. There is a lift, step-free access from the street, and a paid car park next to the building. The nearest tram stop is Example Square, five minutes on foot.

## Phone and urgent contact
The reception desk answers calls during opening hours. For a medical emergency do not wait for the clinic: call the national emergency number or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
