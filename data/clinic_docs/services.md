# Services and specialties

> Sample data: fictional clinic, invented details.

## Specialties
The clinic offers general medicine, dentistry, pediatrics and dermatology. Each appointment lasts 30 minutes.

## General medicine
Two general practitioners, Dr. Amrani and Dr. Benali, handle check-ups, prescriptions renewals, certificates, vaccinations for adults, blood pressure follow-up and common illnesses such as flu or infections.

## Dentistry
Dr. Chaoui offers dental check-ups, cleaning, fillings and tooth extraction. Orthodontics, braces and dental implants are not available at this clinic.

## Pediatrics
Dr. Djerbi sees children from birth to 15 years old. Routine child vaccinations and growth checks are done here. A parent or legal guardian must attend every visit.

## Dermatology
Dr. Ferhat treats acne, eczema, allergies of the skin and moles checks. Cosmetic procedures such as laser hair removal are not offered.

## Laboratory tests
Basic blood tests and urine tests can be collected on site on weekdays before 11:00. Results are sent to the doctor within two working days.
