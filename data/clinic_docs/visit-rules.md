# Visit rules and accessibility

> Sample data: fictional clinic, invented rules.

## Companions
One companion may accompany an adult patient. Children under 15 must come with a parent or guardian. Please keep other visitors outside to keep the waiting room calm.

## Masks and hygiene
Masks are recommended for patients with a cough or fever. Hand sanitiser is available at the entrance.

## Interpreters and languages
Staff speak Arabic, French and English. Sign language interpreting is not available, but a companion may help.

## Accessibility
The clinic has a wheelchair-accessible entrance, lift and toilet. Please tell the reception desk in advance if you need extra help.

## Feedback and complaints
Feedback can be given at the reception desk or by asking the assistant to connect you with a staff member. Complaints are answered within five working days.
