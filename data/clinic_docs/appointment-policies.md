# Appointments, cancellations and late arrivals

> Sample data: fictional clinic, invented policies.

## Booking
Appointments can be booked by phone with the voice assistant or at the reception desk, up to 90 days ahead. The assistant needs the specialty, the day, the time, the patient's name and a phone number.

## Cancellation policy
An appointment can be cancelled free of charge until 2 hours before it starts. Cancelling later, or not showing up, is recorded on the file; after three missed appointments the clinic may ask for a deposit on the next booking.

## Rescheduling
To change an appointment, cancel it and book a new slot. The old slot is released immediately.

## Late arrivals
Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to rebook, so that the next patients are not delayed. Please call if you are running late.

## What to bring
Bring an identity document, the CHIFA card if you have one, previous test results and the list of medicines you currently take. For children bring the vaccination booklet.

## Waiting time
After checking in at the reception desk the average waiting time is about ten minutes. Emergencies may delay the doctor.
