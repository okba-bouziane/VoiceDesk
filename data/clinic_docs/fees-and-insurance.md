# Fees, payment and insurance

> Sample data: fictional clinic, invented prices.

## Consultation fees
A general medicine consultation costs 2,500 dinars. A specialist consultation (dentistry, pediatrics or dermatology) costs 3,500 dinars. A follow-up visit within 14 days of the first one costs half the normal fee.

## Payment methods
The clinic accepts cash and bank cards at the reception desk. Cheques and bank transfers are not accepted. A receipt is always provided.

## Health insurance
The clinic works with the national social security card (CHIFA card) for general medicine and pediatrics. Bring the card and an identity document. Dentistry and dermatology are partly reimbursed depending on the plan, so ask your insurer first.

## Private insurance
Private insurance is not billed directly. The clinic gives a detailed invoice that the patient can send to the insurer for reimbursement.

## Uninsured patients
Patients without insurance pay the full fee on the day. There is no deposit when booking an appointment.
